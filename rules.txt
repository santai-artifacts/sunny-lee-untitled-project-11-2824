2048 — RULES
============

GOAL
----
Combine numbered tiles by sliding them together until you create a tile
with the value 2048. You can keep playing after that to reach even higher
tiles and a bigger score.

THE BOARD
---------
- The board is a 4 x 4 grid.
- The game starts with two tiles, each showing a 2 or a 4.

MAKING A MOVE
-------------
- On each move, every tile slides as far as it can in the direction you
  choose: up, down, left, or right.
- All tiles move at the same time.
- After every move, one new tile appears in a random empty cell. It is a
  2 most of the time, and occasionally a 4.

MERGING TILES
-------------
- When two tiles with the SAME number collide, they merge into a single
  tile worth double the value (2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, and so on).
- A tile can only merge once per move. Three matching tiles in a row will
  merge the pair furthest in the direction of travel, leaving the third.
- The value of each new merged tile is added to your score.

WINNING
-------
- You win the moment a 2048 tile is created.
- You may choose "Keep going" to continue building larger tiles and a
  higher score.

GAME OVER
---------
- The game ends when the board is full AND there are no adjacent tiles
  with matching values left to merge (no possible moves remain).

CONTROLS
--------
- Arrow keys .......... move tiles
- W / A / S / D ....... move tiles
- H / J / K / L ....... move tiles (vim style)
- Swipe .............. move tiles on touch devices
- "New Game" button .. restart at any time

SCORING
-------
- Your score increases by the value of each tile produced by a merge.
- "Best" tracks your highest score and is saved between sessions.

TIP
---
Pick a corner and keep your largest tile there. Build your tiles in order
along one edge and avoid spreading big tiles across the board.
